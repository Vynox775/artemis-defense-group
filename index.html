Our story

> How Artemis began

Artemis was founded on October 19, 2026 as a semi-milsim faction in BRM5. Commanding General Vynoxx775 built it around structure and teamwork: clear ranks, trained operators and a plan before every deployment. Today four divisions answer to one command, and enlistment never closes.

Commanding General & Founder of Artemis

Vynoxx775 founded Artemis on October 19, 2026 with one goal: a faction that plays like a real unit. Built on discipline, structure and teamwork, Artemis runs on a clear chain of command and trained operators. Every deployment starts with a plan, and every operator answers to one command.
